Refund Policy

General information about project cancellations, payments and refund requests.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Overview

Elite Digital Consulting provides professional technology consulting, software development, design, project management and related digital services. Because each engagement may involve different deliverables, resources and timelines, payment and refund arrangements should be specified in the relevant project agreement.

2. Project Deposits

Some projects may require an advance payment or deposit before work begins. Whether a deposit is refundable, and under what conditions, depends on the agreed contract terms, work already undertaken, committed costs and applicable law.

3. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a client requests cancellation before project work starts, we will review the agreement and any costs already incurred. Where a refund is due under the agreement or applicable law, we will communicate the amount and process.

4. Cancellation After Work Has Started

If a project is cancelled after work has begun, the parties should review completed work, approved milestones, committed third-party expenses and any applicable contractual cancellation provisions.

Refund eligibility will be assessed against the agreed terms and applicable legal requirements.

5. Completed Work and Delivered Services

Services already performed or deliverables already accepted may not be eligible for a refund except where the project agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

6. Third-Party Costs

Hosting, domains, software subscriptions, paid licenses, advertising spend and other third-party expenses may be subject to the provider's own cancellation and refund rules. Any treatment of these costs should be set out in the project agreement.

7. Project Delays and Scope Changes

If a project experiences delays or changes in scope, we encourage both parties to discuss revised expectations, deliverables and milestones. Any resulting payment adjustments should be documented in writing.

8. Requesting a Refund Review

To request a review, contact us by email and include:

  • Your name and business name, if applicable.
  • The project or invoice reference.
  • The reason for the request.
  • Relevant payment details and supporting documentation.

Email your request to info@elitedigitalglobalconsulting.com.

9. Review and Resolution

We will review the information provided, the relevant agreement and applicable legal requirements. We may request additional details before communicating the outcome.

Any approved refund will be processed through an agreed, appropriate payment method. Processing times may depend on the payment provider.

10. Your Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or limit rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Where a signed agreement contains more specific provisions, those provisions apply to the extent legally permitted.
