Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our technology services and project delivery.

General Questions

What does Elite Digital Consulting do?

We provide technology consulting, custom website development, web and mobile applications, enterprise software, DevOps, cloud support, AI automation, technical project leadership and ongoing digital platform maintenance.

Where is Elite Digital Consulting located?

Our business location is Tina Johnson Close, Coastal Road, Salem Okun-Ajah, beside Avista Beach, Lekki-Epe Expressway, Eti-Osa LGA, Lagos 106104, Nigeria. We also support remote project discussions with clients in other locations.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Our approach combines international professional experience with engineering and DevOps capabilities based in Nigeria. We can discuss remote collaboration arrangements depending on the project.

Do you work with startups and established companies?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from startups, growing businesses, agencies, institutions and established organizations.

Services and Project Delivery

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. We develop business websites, responsive digital experiences, CMS-based websites, custom web applications and other online platforms based on your requirements.

Do you develop mobile applications?

Yes. We can discuss mobile app projects, including iOS, Android and cross-platform development, as well as application integrations and ongoing improvements.

Can you improve an existing website or application?

Yes. We can assess an existing solution and discuss redesign, modernization, performance improvements, integrations, maintenance and additional functionality.

Do you offer AI and automation solutions?

Yes. We explore practical AI-enabled workflows, business process automation, knowledge assistants and AI integrations where they are appropriate for the client's objectives.

Can you provide DevOps and cloud engineering support?

Yes. Our capabilities include deployment workflows, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure planning, monitoring and technical operations support, subject to project requirements.

Can you support an agency's existing delivery team?

Yes. We can discuss defined technical workstreams, development support, DevOps capacity and technical project coordination alongside existing teams.

Pricing and Communication

How much does a project cost?

Project pricing depends on scope, complexity, integrations, required expertise, delivery timeline and support needs. We discuss your requirements before preparing a suitable proposal.

How long does it take to complete a project?

Timelines vary by project. A focused website may require less time than a custom application or enterprise platform. We discuss milestones and delivery expectations during planning.

How do I request a quotation?

Visit our Contact page and provide your project objectives, expected functionality and any relevant timeline. We can then discuss the next steps and quotation process.

How can I contact your team?

Email info@elitedigitalglobalconsulting.com or message our consulting team on WhatsApp at +234 810 991 7302. You can also use the project enquiry form on our Contact page.

Do you offer support after a website or application launches?

Yes. Maintenance, updates, troubleshooting and ongoing development can be discussed as part of the original project or a separate support arrangement.

What is your refund policy?

Refunds, cancellations and payments depend on the terms of the specific project agreement, the work already completed and applicable law. Please review our Refund Policy page for general information.
