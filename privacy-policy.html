Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information when you interact with our business.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

Elite Digital Consulting respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit our website or communicate with us about our technology consulting and software development services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Your business name and project requirements.
  • Information included in emails, WhatsApp messages or project enquiries.
  • Technical information made available through website hosting and related services.

3. How We Use Information

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, discuss projects, prepare proposals, deliver agreed services, maintain business relationships and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal Grounds for Processing

Where required by applicable data protection law, we process personal information based on appropriate grounds, such as consent, steps requested before entering into a contract, contractual necessity, legitimate business interests or legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, embedded maps, external images and communication links. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Clicking an external link, including WhatsApp or Google Maps, takes you to a separate service.

6. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, professional advisers or project collaborators when reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate safeguards, or where disclosure is legally required.

7. International Data Transfers

Because our business may work with international clients and service providers, some information may be processed across national borders. Where applicable law requires safeguards for such transfers, we seek to use appropriate measures.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including project administration, contractual obligations, legitimate business records and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws applicable to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other action regarding your personal information. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. However, no internet-based system or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology practices or applicable law. The latest version will be published on this page.
